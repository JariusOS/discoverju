# Juju Explorer

Create a discovery app called juju Africa. 2 modes. Search and explore. Explore shows maps, GIS, Google-earth like navigation around Africa. Create special well structured pages for each African country, add every relevant data for each country. 

### Comprehensive Country Template Outline and Indicator Definitions  

>

To evaluate all 54 sovereign African nations consistently, this report employs a standardized 13-parameter data architecture. The template outline and indicator definitions are established as follows:  

* **Official Country Name**: The full, constitutionally recognized name of the sovereign nation.  

* **Geographic Sub-Region**: Regional classification designated by the United Nations and African Union (North Africa, West Africa, Central Africa, East Africa, Southern Africa).  

* **Primary Political Subdivisions**: The count, structure, and formal legal designation of the country's first-tier administrative divisions (e.g., states, provinces, governorates, regions, counties).  

* **Total Population**: National population estimate derived from international demographic database baselines.  

* **Capital City & Population**: Designated political and administrative capital city alongside its resident population.  

* **Most Populated City & Population**: The primary commercial hub or largest metropolitan urban center by population scale.  

* **Gross Domestic Product (GDP) & Per Capita**: Nominal GDP expressed in current United States Dollars (USD), paired with nominal per-capita GDP.  

* **Monthly Statutory Minimum Wage (USD)**: Official legal baseline monthly compensation converted to USD.  

* **Life Expectancy at Birth**: Average life expectancy in years for a newborn based on mortality indicators.  

* **Youth Demographic Profile (% Under 30)**: Proportion of the total national population under 30 years of age.  

* **Poverty Headcount Ratio (%)**: Percentage of the population living below national poverty baselines or international poverty metrics.  

* **Adult Literacy & Educated %**: Percentage of the adult population aged 15 and above capable of reading and writing.  

* **Sex Ratio**: Ratio of males to females within the population (expressed as males per female). 

* **Aligned Structural Parameter**: A relevant contextual development metric, such as Urbanization Rate, Key Export Share, or Primary Sector Employment Contribution.
